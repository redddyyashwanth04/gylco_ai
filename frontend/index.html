How it works

Two modes, each with an explanation.

Current assessment

Enter today's measurements. The model returns estimated risk for hypertension, nephropathy and cardiovascular outcomes, the factors driving each estimate (SHAP), and a plain-language summary.

Progression forecast

View a glucose trajectory from a tracked patient or a pre-loaded MIMIC-IV demo case, with the model's next-reading forecast plotted after the last observation, along with its typical error.

Your judgment stays in charge

Outputs are estimates and points for review. The clinician makes every decision.
